\chapter{Introduction}\label{chap:introduction}

Bayesian optimization (BO) is a widely used framework for optimizing expensive-to-evaluate, black-box functions. It has been successfully applied to a wide range of problems, including hyperparameter tuning \citep{pmlr-v54-klein17a, WU201926}, materials discovery \citep{10.1039/c9sc04026a, 10.1039/d2nr07147a} and robotics \citep{calandra2016bayesian, berkenkamp2023bayesian}. BO typically employs a surrogate model to approximate the objective function and an acquisition function to determine the next point to evaluate. The surrogate model provides both predicted mean values and uncertainty estimates, allowing the acquisition function to effectively balance exploration and exploitation.
